Hardware Architecture Summary

The Raspberry Pi collects video and mic audio from shirt accessories and sends it over a direct laptop hotspot network. The laptop runs YOLO object tracking, SQLite spatial logging, and offline speech synthesis locally.

Raspberry Pi 4 / 5

Pocket Processing & Audio Node

Primary Role

Acts as the wearable pocket hub. Receives shirt camera frames via USB, inputs collar mic audio, outputs voice through 3.5mm audio jack, and streams data over local wireless to the laptop.
